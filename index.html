Real image pixels on official MediaPipe triangles. Open borders reveal gaps; highlighted eye/lip contours are model landmarks, not reconstructed eyeballs. Relative Z isn't measured depth; eye fills, mouth rig, hair segmentation and occlusion remain future modules.

What's real? Detected landmarks, official triangles, the original photo texture and Three.js rendering. What's not here? AI-estimated depth, hair/neck segmentation, hidden head surfaces or realistic blinking.

Model assets are downloaded on first analysis. Device speed, browser support and network access affect success. We never quietly substitute a simulated result.
